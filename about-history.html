About Us

History

Our history

Built on experience.
Growing worldwide.

From our predecessor's beginnings in 1998 to an international manufacturing business, explore the milestones behind GLTURBO.

  1. 1998

    The beginning

    Our predecessor was founded in June to manufacture valves and blowers for wastewater treatment.

  2. 2006

    The GL Series takes shape

    Design of the integrally geared turbo blower began in June with an industry team in Boston.

  3. 2008

    GLTURBO is established

    In May, the blower business became a separate company focused on turbo blower design and manufacturing.

  4. 2015

    International expansion

    The 200th turbo blower was manufactured in March. GLTURBO International ApS was established in Denmark in October, followed by the Lone Star Blower partnership in Houston in November.

  5. 2016

    A broader manufacturing platform

    GLTURBO China completed its IPO in January. The LS Series launched in April, and the Houston manufacturing facility was established in September.

  6. 2017

    300 units sold

    GLTURBO reached its 300th unit sold in January.
