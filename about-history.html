About Us

History

Our history

Built on experience.
Growing worldwide.

From our predecessor's beginnings in 1998 and GLTURBO's founding in Boston in 2006 to an international blower and precision control business, explore the milestones behind GLTURBO.

  1. 1998

    The beginning

    Our predecessor was founded in June to manufacture valves and blowers for wastewater treatment.

  2. 2006

    Founded in Boston

    GLTURBO was founded in Boston, USA, in June to develop single-stage geared turbo blower technology.

  3. 2007

    The first GL blower

    The first GL blower passed its overspeed test in December.

  4. 2008

    Operations established in China

    GLTURBO China was established in Wuxi in May. The first GL blower entered service in Dongting, Wuxi, in December.

  5. 2014

    Precision aeration enters service

    GLTURBO's first precision aeration system entered service in Tongliang, Chongqing, in June.

  6. 2015

    International expansion

    The 200th turbo blower was manufactured in March. GLTURBO International ApS was established in Denmark in October, followed by the Lone Star Blower partnership in Houston in November.

  7. 2016

    A broader manufacturing platform

    GLTURBO China completed its IPO in January. The LS Series launched in April, and the Houston manufacturing facility was established in September.

  8. 2017

    300 units sold

    GLTURBO reached its 300th unit sold in January.

  9. 2018

    New technology and European operations

    The first DT blower entered service in the USA in April. GLTURBO Europe was established in Leipzig, Germany, in November.

  10. 2019

    GLTURBO India

    GLTURBO India was established in Bengaluru in May.

  11. 2021

    The first GA installation

    The first GA blower entered service in Huishan, Wuxi, in November.

  12. 2022

    BioChem technology joins GLTURBO

    GLTURBO acquired the assets of US-based BioChem Technology, Inc. in August, extending the company's precision aeration control expertise.
