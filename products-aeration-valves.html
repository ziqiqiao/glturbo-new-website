Aeration Control Valves

Diamond Valve

Model-based control of aeration air.

DN 100–400 | wafer connection | air service

Diamond Valve calculates the valve opening from a flow model, with PID adjustments to support precise aeration control.

  • Intelligent multi-turn actuator with a 4–20 mA control signal.
  • 380 V / 50 Hz actuator supply in the published configuration.
  • Stainless steel valve plate with CF8 valve bodies.
  • Designed for integration into precision aeration control systems.

MassTrol Valve

Measure and control airflow in one valve.

DN 200–700 | flanged connection | integrated flow measurement

MassTrol combines valve positioning with airflow measurement to help address installations where the straight pipe length needed by a conventional thermal mass flowmeter is difficult to provide.

  • Accepts either a 4–20 mA valve-opening signal or a 4–20 mA airflow signal.
  • The valve HMI displays both opening position and mass flow.
  • 220 V / 50 Hz intelligent multi-turn actuator in the published configuration.
  • The centered opening supports the integrated flow measurement method.

Measurement accuracy: the brochure specifies ±1.5% with 5 pipe diameters of straight pipe upstream and 3 downstream. Confirm performance for other installation layouts with GLTURBO.
