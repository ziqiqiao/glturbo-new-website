Research & Development

Engineered as a whole.

Our engineering team develops single-stage centrifugal blowers and GPAC precision aeration control as an integrated system, matching blower performance to changing process demand.

  • Integrated aerodynamic design of impellers, shrouds, volutes, inlet guide vanes, and variable vane diffusers
  • Solid modeling, finite element analysis, and computational fluid dynamics
  • Coordinated inlet and outlet guide vane control for efficient airflow regulation
  • Activated Sludge Model-based feedforward control, dynamic dissolved oxygen setpoints, and airflow distribution

GLTURBO's 2022 acquisition of BioChem Technology assets expanded our expertise in wastewater process modeling and precision aeration control.
