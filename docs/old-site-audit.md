## Company Story

Core company message:

- Founded in Boston, USA.
- Global business with manufacturing plants in the USA, China, and Denmark.
- Sales and service representatives in more than 30 countries.

Timeline highlights:

- 2017: 300th unit sold.

## Product Lines

### GL Series Integrally Geared Single Stage Turbo Blowers

Published range:

- Power: 75 to 4,500 kW
- Flow and pressure: up to 120,000 m3/h and 3 bar

Features:

- High aerodynamic efficiency across the flow range
- Constant or variable speed configurations
- Turndown to 40% of design flow at constant speed
- Variable prerotational inlet guide vanes and discharge diffuser vanes
- Sleeve bearings for long life and reduced maintenance
- Oil-free compression
- Low operating and maintenance costs
- API 617 compliant options

Sales message:

- "Try Before You Buy"
- Offers energy analysis and site survey for potential no-obligation trials.

## Initial New Website Direction

Recommended primary audiences:

- Municipal water and wastewater engineers
- EPC and consulting engineering firms
- Industrial plant operators
- Procurement teams evaluating blower lifecycle cost
- Service and retrofit customers

Recommended homepage structure:

- Hero focused on high-efficiency blower systems, not generic corporate messaging
- Product line comparison for GL, LS, controls, packaged systems, and service
- Application-focused section led by water and wastewater
- Proof points: global manufacturing, 30+ country service network, energy analysis, long design heritage
- Contact/request quote section with product/service routing

Recommended tone:

- Industrial, precise, engineering-led
- More modern and easier to scan than the old site
- Emphasize efficiency, reliability, lifecycle cost, and single-source responsibility

Open decisions:

- Whether the new site should be English-only, Chinese-only, or bilingual.
- Whether to preserve all legacy pages or consolidate into fewer stronger pages.
- Whether to reuse old product images or create a refreshed visual system.
- Whether contact forms will submit to an existing backend, email service, CRM, or static form provider.
