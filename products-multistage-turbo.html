LS Multistage Turbo

LS Series

Multistage Centrifugal Turbo Blowers

25 to 2,200 kW | 500 to 7,500 m3/hr | up to 1.7 bar delta pressure

A modern multistage blower design for air or gas, pressure or vacuum service. Each model can be built with one to ten stages, and multiple impeller designs can be combined to maximize efficiency.

  • Pressure and exhauster configurations.
  • Cast casing and individually selected impellers.
  • Labyrinth seals and robust shaft design.
  • Pulse-free airflow and packaged system options.

Core Technology

Casing, impellers, shaft, bearings, seals, and inlet vanes engineered as a system.

Pressure or Vacuum

Flexible staging supports a wide range of industrial duties.

Packaged Components

Controls, starters, sensors, valves, silencers, and instrumentation.
