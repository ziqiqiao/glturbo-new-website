Customer Service

Customer Service

Support throughout your equipment's life.

From blower selection and GPAC configuration to installation and ongoing maintenance, GLTURBO supports the complete operating life of your equipment.

  • Before purchase: application review, product selection, and matching auxiliary equipment to your process requirements.
  • Installation and commissioning: installation guidance, instrument and meter testing, and maintenance technician training.
  • After-sales support: regular maintenance, remote monitoring, fault diagnosis and recovery, and spare parts supply.

Our service commitment is to respond within 24 hours of receiving a customer service request.

Spare parts and equipment records

GLTURBO maintains permanent records for its blowers to support parts identification and service. Available parts include tilting-pad and hydrodynamic bearings, impellers, air filter elements, labyrinth and bearing oil seals, and inlet guide vane and variable vane diffuser actuators.
