GLTURBO Company

Global engineering with local support.

Headquartered in Boston, GLTURBO brings more than 20 years of experience in environmental protection and energy-efficient technologies. Our operations span Wuxi, Boston, Houston, Copenhagen, Leipzig, and Bangalore.

Our solutions serve more than 200 wastewater treatment projects across over 20 countries. In 2022, GLTURBO acquired the assets of BioChem Technology, Inc., expanding our expertise in precision aeration control.
