GLTURBO designs and manufactures single-stage high-speed centrifugal blowers for municipal and industrial wastewater treatment, power generation, chemical processing, pharmaceuticals, and food and beverage manufacturing.

Our operations in Boston, Houston, Wuxi, Leipzig, Copenhagen, and Bengaluru bring together research, manufacturing, and lifecycle services. GLTURBO solutions have been deployed in more than 200 wastewater treatment projects across over 20 countries, including the United States, Canada, Singapore, China, and India.

In 2022, GLTURBO acquired the assets of BioChem Technology, Inc., strengthening the GPAC precision aeration control platform. GPAC combines process modeling, blower control, and airflow distribution to help wastewater plants manage dissolved oxygen and energy use.
