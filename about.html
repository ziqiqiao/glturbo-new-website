Company

Founded in Boston, serving projects worldwide.

GLTURBO is a global business that designs and manufactures high-efficiency centrifugal blowers for low-pressure compressed air or gas in pressure and vacuum applications.

Our engineers bring more than 200 years of combined centrifugal compressor experience. The team works across product design, application engineering, packaging, controls, testing, commissioning, and service.

Why GLTURBO

Because customer service matters.

GLTURBO works as a single source for blower systems, combining efficient equipment with application knowledge, controls, packaging, and lifecycle support.

One Source

One team coordinates the blower, motor, controls, valves, instrumentation, packaging, testing, and service.

Customer Service

Support begins with selection and continues through installation, training, monitoring, and field service.

Lifecycle Performance

Efficiency, turndown, reliability, maintenance, and process controls are considered across the life of the system.

Research & Development

Developing efficient geared and direct-driven centrifugal blowers.

GLTURBO uses modern engineering software to build solid models, perform finite element analysis, verify performance through computational fluid dynamics, and manufacture modern product designs.

The team also designs control systems, motor control systems, and complete packaged systems, including both new products and modifications to current designs.

Testing & Certifications

Manufactured, packaged, and tested to international standards.

GLTURBO measures performance on compliant test stands and supports performance testing to ASME PTC 9, ASME PTC 10, ISO 5389, and ISO 1217.

Project certifications can be provided to meet applicable global standards.

History

Company milestones

  1. 1998The predecessor company was founded to manufacture valves and blowers for wastewater treatment.
  2. 2006Design of the GL Series integrally geared turbo blower began in Boston.
  3. 2008The blower business became the separate GLTURBO company.
  4. 2015The 200th turbo blower was manufactured, GLTURBO International ApS was established in Denmark, and a partnership with Lone Star Blower was formed in Houston.
  5. 2016China IPO, launch of the LS Series, and creation of the Houston manufacturing facility.
  6. 2017The 300th unit was sold.
